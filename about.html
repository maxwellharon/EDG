About Us

Driving Social Enterprise for the SDGs

Eden Destiny Group is a collective of technical experts and partners dedicated to empowering stakeholders in the public and private sectors. Our mission is to accelerate the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals through social enterprise, creative economy, wellness, and education.

Our Mission

To empower communities by providing strategic support, fostering inclusion, and building sustainable livelihoods, with a special focus on women and youth.
