Education Destiny

Empowering Through Learning

Education Destiny is the strategic advisory arm that supports the Prof. Olewe Nyunya Foundation. The Foundation's flagship project is the J.Don Academy, a school built in honour of the Founder's late father, Prof. Olewe Nyunya, a career academician who trained diplomats.

We believe that education is the cornerstone of sustainable development. J.Don Academy provides quality learning rooted in values, innovation, and global citizenship, preparing the next generation of African leaders.

A Joint Initiative

HEAL. READ. GROW.

Empowering ALL Youth in Underserved Communities Through Creativity, Literacy & Education

Eden Destiny Group (EDG) & The Books & Trunks Society (B&T)

Pilot Programme • Gacharage Schools, Kiambu • Q2 2026

A structured 2026 pilot uniting two organisations around a single, urgent mission: to equip youth with emotional resilience, creative expression, and literacy skills, and to protect them from mental health collapse and addiction.

1 in 4
Young Kenyans face mental health challenges (WHO, 2020)
10%
Youth aged 15–24 report drug use (KNBS, 2022)
Girls
Face compounded barriers: education gaps, inadequate menstrual health support, lack of creative & emotional outlets
🎵

HEAL

Led by Eden Destiny Group

Music therapy, dance, storytelling, sports for mental health, and values education, all facilitated by trained professionals and rooted in African cultural heritage.

Sustainability: Structured procedures will enable school staff and community volunteers to continue activities beyond the pilot period.

📚

READ & GROW

Led by The Books & Trunks Society

Story reading sessions, book club activities, and donated reading material, establishing enriched reading corners at Gacharage Schools and linking emotional expression with literacy identity.

Sustainability: Book clubs and reading corners handed over to trained teacher-champions, with quarterly volunteer check-ins by B&T.

Pilot Event , Gacharage Schools, Kiambu

The first pilot will be held across the Gacharage Schools campus, serving the Kindergarten, Primary School, and High School, in Kiambu County, during Q2 2026. A full-day immersive programme for both girls and boys:

  • Registration, welcome gathering & community orientation
  • Music therapy & storytelling workshops led by EDG facilitators
  • Sports for mental health activities
  • Dance sessions with cultural grounding
  • Story reading & book club session led by B&T volunteers
  • Values education & emotional resilience module
  • B&T reading corner setup & book donation ceremony
  • Closing reflection, testimonials & community prayer

Expected Impact

Short-term outcomes from Gacharage pilot:

  • Pilot completed with documented outcomes
  • Young minds engaged in structured creative & emotional wellness sessions
  • School libraries enriched with donated books & new reading corners
  • Documented testimonials from school leadership on student mental health realities
  • Replicable event model ready for scale across Kiambu and beyond

Medium-to-long-term vision:

  • Reduced absenteeism & improved emotional well-being among participants
  • Sustained community book clubs anchored by teacher-champions
  • A permanent EDG–B&T alliance offered to other schools and counties

Implementation Timeline

Phase 1 , Now to Mid Q2 2026

Sign MOU • Finalise school schedule • Source books • Train facilitators

Phase 2 , Mid/Late Q2 2026

Execute Gacharage pilot event • Collect testimonials from teachers & board members • Administer M&E surveys

Milestone: Impact report launched; 2027 roadmap drafted

Phase 3 , Q3–Q4 2026

Publish impact report • Donor engagement • Plan 2027 expansion to additional schools

Legal & Safeguarding Commitments
  • All sessions follow child-safe, culturally rooted facilitation standards
  • No personally identifying information about child participants will be shared publicly without explicit parental or guardian consent
  • A Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) will be signed by EDG and B&T before any funds are raised or spent
  • Transparent financial records will be maintained and accessible to both partners
  • All donated books will be age-appropriate and vetted by community liaisons before distribution
