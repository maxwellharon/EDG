Education Destiny

Empowering Through Learning

Education Destiny is the strategic advisory arm that supports the Prof. Olewe Nyunya Foundation. The Foundation's flagship project is the J.Don Academy, a school built in honour of the Founder's late father, Prof. Olewe Nyunya, a career academician who trained diplomats.

We believe that education is the cornerstone of sustainable development. J.Don Academy provides quality learning rooted in values, innovation, and global citizenship, preparing the next generation of African leaders.
