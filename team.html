The People Behind EDG

Meet Our Dedicated Experts

Eden Destiny Group is powered by a passionate, multi‑disciplinary team committed to driving the SDGs through social enterprise. Tap any profile to discover their full story and expertise.

Roselyne Olewe

Founder & Managing Director

About Roselyne

Roselyne is a seasoned professional with 30 years experience in the private sector in blue‑chip FMCGs and the NGO sector. Her expertise spans Brand Management, Advertising, Programme Management, Business Development, Consortium Management, Government Relations, Policy and Advocacy, Labour Rights, Disability Inclusion, and the Creative Economy.

She currently serves as the Executive Director of Eden Destiny Group Ltd and is a Board Member of the Talanta Mtaani Youth Empowerment Programme. A passionate mental health advocate, she drives EDG’s holistic wellness initiatives.

Elizabeth Mutheu Mutemi

Risk Finance & Compliance

About Elizabeth

Elizabeth is a seasoned Finance Executive and Strategic Leader, driving financial stewardship and growth in complex environments. She leverages expertise in financial governance, risk management, grants compliance, and results‑based management.

With a proven track record of advising leadership and boards, she excels in strategic planning, program scale‑up, and team building. Proficient in Microsoft Dynamics Navision, QuickBooks, and ZOHO, she is committed to transparency, accountability, and organisational sustainability.

Hezekia Watta

Creative Director

About Hezekia

Hezekia Watta is a visionary creative leader with over 15 years of experience in branding, multimedia production, and strategic communication across East Africa. He has led creative campaigns for corporates, NGOs, government institutions, and international organisations.

His expertise spans creative strategy, branding, and multimedia production. He combines creativity and technology to transform ideas into powerful experiences.

Lisa Beryl Ondiek

Program Director

About Lisa

Lisa is an experienced Program Manager with a demonstrated history of working in the non‑profit organisation management industry. She is skilled in Microsoft Word, Accounting, Java, Financial Accounting, and Teamwork.

A strong program and project management professional, she holds a Bachelor of Commerce (BCom) focused on Business Administration and Management, with an entrepreneurship specialisation from Strathmore University.

Joab Okore

Cultural Editor

About Joab

Joab Okore made history as the first male from Kisumu to receive the Presidential Head of State Commendation (HSC) award, recognised for his COVID‑19 response efforts and philanthropic work at the 2021 Mashujaa Day celebrations in Kirinyaga County.

Through his company, he mobilised resources to distribute masks and sanitisers to schools and public gatherings. Joab champions peace, education, and cultural preservation, stressing the importance of local languages and traditions in alignment with Article 7 of the Constitution.

Bernard Nyasanga

Community Mobilizer

About Bernard

Bernard is an accomplished educationist and community mobiliser with over ten years of experience since 2010. He has served across Nakuru, Kisumu, Kajiado, Dubai (UAE), and currently Nairobi County.

He specialises in quality assurance, curriculum development, and school management within both local and international education systems. Bernard holds an Honours Degree in Linguistics and Sociology (1996) and a Postgraduate Diploma in Education from Egerton University (2001).

Kristian Daniel Onyango

Architecture & Creative

About Kristian

Kristian is a detail‑oriented Graduate Architect specialising in design and project management. He leverages his architectural skills to drive collaborative projects.

As a Recording Artist and Vocal Post‑Production Engineer, he excels in audio production (FL Studio), workflow optimisation, and creative direction. His dual pursuits in architecture and music enhance conceptualisation and innovative problem‑solving. He holds Bachelor of Architecture and Bachelor of Architectural Studies degrees from the University of Nairobi.

Kraig Dixon Ochieng

Data Scientist & Data Analyst

About Kraig

Kraig is a skilled Data Scientist specialising in building scalable machine learning solutions, predictive models, and AI agent systems. He drives production deployments, explainable AI, and data visualisation.

His work spans healthcare integrations, workflow automation, and cloud deployments. Kraig graduated with a First Class Honours, BS Computer Science from the University of Nairobi.
