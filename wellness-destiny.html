Wellness Destiny

Holistic Health for All

Wellness Destiny is dedicated to promoting wholistic wellness through events that bring together stakeholders from the wellness industry. Wellness is an active process of becoming aware of and making choices toward a healthy and fulfilling life.

Mental health is a cornerstone of our work. Our founder is a passionate mental health advocate, and we design programmes that address physical, mental, and social well‑being.

Wellness Destiny Programme Overview

A structured mental health and wellness intervention designed for scalability and measurable impact.

Problem Statement

Kenya faces a severe mental health crisis: 1 in 4 Kenyans experiences a mental health condition, yet only 10% access care. Stigma, lack of affordable services, and limited trained professionals leave millions untreated, affecting productivity, family stability, and community safety.

Target Beneficiaries

Youth (18-35) Women Corporate Employees Persons with Disabilities Rural Communities

Priority: Underserved populations in 18 counties, with a focus on low-income urban settlements and remote rural areas.

Geographic Coverage

Current: 18 counties including Nairobi, Kisumu, Kiambu, Mombasa, Kisii, Nakuru
Planned (2026-2027): Expansion to all 47 counties + virtual national reach via Serene Mind platform.

Expected Outcomes

  • 30% reduction in self-reported anxiety/depression symptoms among beneficiaries
  • 5,000+ individuals reached annually through expos and workshops
  • 100+ community mental health champions trained
  • 20+ corporate wellness programmes implemented
  • Sustainable virtual therapy platform (Serene Mind) serving 10,000+ users by 2027

Budget & Funding Needs

Annual programme budget: KSh 8.5 million (~USD 65,000)

Funding gap: KSh 5.2 million (~USD 40,000)

Use of funds: Wellness expos (35%), therapist stipends (25%), community training (20%), M&E (10%), operational (10%)

What We Do

Our Wellness Focus

Mental Health Advocacy

Championing mental health awareness, breaking stigma, and providing resources for psychological well‑being. We run campaigns, workshops, and support networks.

Wellness Expos & Events

Annual expos that connect fitness experts, nutritionists, therapists, and wellness brands with the community. A platform for learning, trying new products, and networking.

Corporate Wellness Programs

Customised wellness solutions for organisations: stress management, team‑building retreats, and mental health first‑aid training to boost employee productivity and happiness.

Virtual Care

Serene Mind

The 5‑Minute Check‑In

5 minutes for KSh 5,000 – A quick, focused session with a specialist.

Our mental wellness doctors are based in Kampala, Uganda but available for virtual meetings worldwide via www.serenemind.app

Step 1: Start Your 5

Book your 5 focused minutes. Keep it intentional – one topic, one goal.

Step 2: Pick Your Specialist

Choose from The Serene Mind's list of specialists. Filter by area of focus.

Step 3: Book Within Their Hours

Select a slot inside the specialist's posted booking hours.

Step 4: Get The Call

Your therapist calls you at the booked time. Be ready at minute 0.

Step 5: Wrap & Next Steps

Session ends after 5 minutes. You'll get notes or recommendations.
